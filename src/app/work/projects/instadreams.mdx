## Overview

[Instadreams](https://instadreams.com) is an alarm clock whose snooze button says **Record dream**. You get your nine minutes; the price is a few mumbled sentences about what you dreamt. By morning the app has transcribed the recording, cleaned it into readable prose, given it a title and painted a cover image keyed to the dream's own details.

## The Problem

Dreams evaporate within minutes of waking. Dream journal apps fail because a half-asleep person at 4am will not open an app and type. The habit never forms, so the journal stays empty.

I have wanted this product since 2012, when I bought the domain and built the first version as a Telegram bot. It had about one and a half real users. The capture step was the problem, and it took speech-to-text, language models and image models to make it buildable.

## The Solution

- **Alarm-first capture**: a real alarm clock on Apple AlarmKit that breaks through Silent mode and Focus, with voice as the only input
- **Saved on device the instant recording stops**, before any network request
- **AI pipeline**: transcription, faithful cleanup, title, one cover image per dream
- **Free and unlimited capture**, with paid comics (3 to 8 panel pages from a dream) and original alarm melodies
- **Private by default**: no feed, no interpretation, no sleep tracking, no training on your content

## Tech Stack

Flutter with native Swift and Kotlin alarm modules, Phoenix backend. Heading to the App Store and Google Play.
