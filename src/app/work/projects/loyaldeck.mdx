## Overview

[LoyalDeck](https://loyaldeck.com) is a digital loyalty platform for any local business with repeat customers. Customers scan a QR code and keep a stamp card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet; the business gets analytics, broadcasts and a reason to message its regulars. [BaristaCard](/work/baristacard) is the coffee vertical, powered by the same engine.

## The Problem

Bakeries, barbers and car washes were signing up for BaristaCard and handing out "Coffee Beans" for croissants and haircuts. About 90% of the engine was already vertical-agnostic. The only thing stopping more of them was the word "coffee" on every screen.

## The Solution

- **One engine, many verticals**: shared stamp cycles, points, levels, wallet passes, QR scanning, billing and payouts, with per-vertical branding, vocabulary and defaults
- **Industry presets** so a nail salon starts with a 5-visit card and a bakery with a 9-stamp card
- **14 industry landing pages** at launch, each answering the question the owner actually searches for
- **Free plan** for a single location, paid tiers for multi-location and advanced mechanics

## Tech Stack

Elixir, Phoenix LiveView, PostgreSQL, Oban, Stripe, Apple PKPass and Google Wallet, deployed on Fly.io. Same codebase as BaristaCard.
